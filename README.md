# Program Plan Training

An interactive training module for new Nightingale College Registrar staff 
learning to build Program Plans from scratch, covering transfer and LPN credit 
application, program curricula, foundational learners, semester pushes, and 
credentialing rules for EL courses.

## Live Training
👉 [Launch Training](https://neomi-merced.github.io/program-plan-training/)

## What's Inside

- 5 briefing sections that build from foundations to program-specific rules
- 9 branching scenarios with 34 decision-based questions built on realistic learner profiles
- Score tracking — earn points for first-try correct answers
- Optional hints on curriculum-heavy questions, available without losing points
- Interactive Course Guide pop-up showing every course's prerequisites, 
  co-requisites, and requisites for the BSN, LPN to ASN, and PN programs
- 25-card interactive flashcard study deck
- Quick Reference guide with all key rules and labels
- Completion tracking through a pre-filled Microsoft Form that records each 
  learner's name and score to an Excel roster
- Feedback form at completion

## Topics Covered

- Program Plan fundamentals — semester sequencing, the standard checklist, and PP notes
- Prerequisites, co-requisites, and requisites, and how they determine course placement
- Condensing plans and maximizing full-time loads without dropping other semesters below 12 credits
- BSN, LPN to ASN, PN, and RN to BSN program structures, including the PN SCI225 rule
- LPN license verification, schedule holds, and labeling LPN, MDC, HESI, and Direct Transfer credits
- Foundational learners (Semester 0, ENG90/MAT90) vs. regular TCE/PP pushes
- Credentialing deadlines and when EL courses can be scheduled

## Design Approach

- **Audience:** New Registrar staff with no prior Program Plan experience
- **Source material:** Consolidated seven separate training decks, the Program Plan 
  checklist, and the nursing curriculum cheat sheet into one guided experience
- **Scaffolding:** Briefings introduce each rule before learners apply it in a scenario, 
  and hints plus the Course Guide give just-in-time support on the hardest questions
- **Realistic distractors:** Wrong answers reflect common processing mistakes, each 
  with feedback explaining why it's wrong
- **Paired scenarios:** Related cases, such as foundational learners vs. regular pushes, 
  test whether learners can tell similar processes apart
- **Measurable outcomes:** First-try scoring and completion tracking show who has 
  finished the training and how well they understood it

## Built With
